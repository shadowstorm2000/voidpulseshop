ABOUT

VoidPulse is one person making original science-fiction artwork and putting it on T-shirts.

Every design starts as a piece of art, not a template. It gets printed and shipped through Zazzle, so ordering, sizing, and returns all work exactly the way they do on any other Zazzle product.

New designs get added as they're ready. No release schedule, no drops calendar — just the shop growing over time.
